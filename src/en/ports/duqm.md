## Where it is and why it matters

Duqm lies in Al Wusta governorate on the Arabian Sea coast, roughly 550 km south of Muscat. The port is part of the Special Economic Zone at Duqm, one of the largest economic zones in the region by area, with a refinery, a dry dock, an industrial area and an airport. Its position outside the Gulf makes it a strategic option for cargo that wants to avoid Hormuz entirely, and a potential call on the main east–west trunk route.

## What it handles

A multipurpose port with deep berths suited to heavy lift, project cargo and bulk, alongside a dry dock for ship repair. Container services exist but are less frequent than at Sohar and Salalah, and grow with the industrial projects in the zone.

## What an importer needs to know

- **Best suited** to project equipment, heavy cargo and shipments bound for Al Wusta, northern Dhofar and the zone's own projects.
- **Road access** to Muscat and the interior is relatively long; price the trucking leg before assuming Duqm is cheaper.
- **Customs and tax incentives** inside the Special Economic Zone differ from the local market; what enters the zone is not treated as an import until it leaves it.
- **Scheduled container services** are fewer, so check sailing schedules before relying on it for regular shipments.

## Who typically uses it

Major project contractors, heavy-industry equipment importers, oil, gas and mining companies operating in central Oman, and investors inside the economic zone.
